# Merax Queen Murphy Bed — Assembly and Scene Blueprint

## Scope and evidence

This document defines the proposed component breakdown, assembly dependency order, and video scene structure for the Merax Queen Murphy Bed with Adjustable Side Storage (Amazon listing B0GQHDLYWB).

The supplied product images and Amazon dimensions define the product geometry. The reference video `0U14ugOw5vw` defines presentation language and pacing only. The sequence below is a reconstruction for visualization, not an official Merax installation manual. Exact fastener counts, hole locations, wall anchors, and piston preload must be checked against the manufacturer manual before real-world assembly.

Verified listing facts:

- Overall width: 94.2 in
- Overall height: 88.5 in
- Open depth: 82 in
- Closed depth: 15.7 in
- Queen mattress compatibility; maximum mattress thickness: 8 in
- MDF and plywood construction
- Five-level adjustable side storage, installable on either side
- Dual-piston folding mechanism
- Maximum listed bed load: 600 lb

The visual reconstruction uses the gray finish shown in the supplied reference images.

## Product component hierarchy

1. Base plinth
2. Outer side upright
3. Bed-bay divider / inner bookcase upright
4. Opposite bed-bay upright
5. Side-storage back panel
6. Side-storage bottom shelf
7. Four adjustable shelves and shelf pins
8. Upper bed-bay header
9. Lower bed-bay crossbar
10. Rear floor brace
11. Full-width top cap

### Group B — Moving bed platform

1. Left side rail
2. Right side rail
3. Head rail
4. Foot rail
5. Longitudinal center support
6. Two deck panels
7. Five transverse mattress slats
8. Six-panel decorative front facade
9. Two pull handles

### Group C — Pivot and lift mechanism

1. Left cabinet pivot bracket
2. Right cabinet pivot bracket
3. Pivot bolts, washers, and locknuts
4. Left gas-piston body and rod
5. Right gas-piston body and rod
6. Left folding leg, foot, hinge, and pivot bolt
7. Right folding leg, foot, hinge, and pivot bolt

### Group D — Final-use items

1. Queen mattress, maximum 8 in thick
2. Mattress retention method — exact strap or stop must be verified
3. Shelf positions and optional decor are non-structural

## Assembly dependency order

```text
Cabinet base
  -> three vertical uprights
  -> bookcase back and adjustable shelves
  -> lower crossbar, rear brace, and upper header
  -> top cap
  -> wall anchoring

Bed perimeter
  -> center support
  -> deck panels and mattress slats
  -> decorative front facade and handles
  -> folding legs

Fixed cabinet + moving bed
  -> left and right pivot brackets
  -> pivot bolts, washers, and locknuts
  -> dual gas pistons
  -> controlled motion test without mattress
  -> mattress installation
  -> final open/close and clearance test
```

Do not install the decorative facade before the pivot and piston close-ups if it blocks tool access or hides the mechanism from the camera.

## Video scene plan

The reference video is 2:35 long. The Merax version needs approximately 2:55–3:15 because it includes the five-level side-storage module and two-sided mechanism details.

### Scene 00 — Cold open and function preview (0:00–0:10)

- Show the completed cabinet in a furnished room.
- Pull both handles; the bed rotates down and both feet unfold.
- Brief close-ups of a pivot bracket, piston, handle, and adjustable shelf.
- English title: `Merax Queen Murphy Bed with Adjustable Side Storage`.

### Scene 01 — Exploded product overview (0:10–0:17)

- Cut to a clean white studio.
- Move all components into a readable exploded arrangement.
- Separate the four major groups with labels: `CABINET`, `SIDE STORAGE`, `BED PLATFORM`, `LIFT MECHANISM`.
- Camera: front three-quarter orbit, then settle to orthographic-like assembly view.

### Scene 02 — Parts and hardware identification (0:17–0:25)

- Present the large panels first, then rails, shelves, pistons, brackets, bolts, washers, and screws.
- Each part receives a short English label and its internal part ID.
- Never display an unverified manufacturer part number.

### Scene 03 — Base and vertical uprights (0:25–0:42)

- Place the base plinth flat.
- Install the outer storage upright.
- Install the storage divider / bed-bay upright.
- Install the opposite bed-bay upright.
- Show one fastener installation in close-up, then repeat the remaining fasteners with a shorter motion.
- End on a square, freestanding U-shaped structure.

### Scene 04 — Side-storage module (0:42–0:58)

- Slide in the back panel.
- Install the bottom shelf.
- Insert shelf pins at matched heights.
- Slide in all four adjustable shelves from bottom to top.
- Show that the storage module may be mirrored left or right, but keep one orientation for the rest of the video.

### Scene 05 — Complete the fixed cabinet (0:58–1:15)

- Install the rear floor brace.
- Install the lower bed-bay crossbar.
- Install the upper bed-bay header.
- Lower the full-width top cap into place.
- Tighten the visible top and base fasteners.
- Result: the fixed cabinet is structurally complete.

### Scene 06 — Anchor cabinet to wall (1:15–1:23)

- Move the cabinet against a wall plane.
- Highlight wall-anchor positions and show anchors entering structural framing.
- English safety card: `ANCHOR TO STRUCTURAL FRAMING. FOLLOW THE OFFICIAL MANUAL.`
- The animation must not invent anchor type, quantity, or spacing.

### Scene 07 — Build the bed-frame perimeter (1:23–1:38)

- Return to the white studio and lay the left and right rails flat.
- Insert head and foot rails to form the rectangle.
- Install the longitudinal center support.
- Show corner fasteners entering along their true axes.

### Scene 08 — Install deck panels and slats (1:38–1:51)

- Lower the left deck panel.
- Lower the right deck panel.
- Place all five pale timber slats one at a time.
- Finish with a top-down shot confirming even spacing.

### Scene 09 — Build the decorative front face (1:51–2:05)

- Flip or rotate the platform to expose the cabinet-facing side.
- Install the six inset panels.
- Install the outer stiles, two mullions, and three horizontal rails.
- Install both pull handles.
- Keep the platform separate from the cabinet during this scene.

### Scene 10 — Install folding legs (2:05–2:16)

- Fit the left and right leg pivots to the foot rail.
- Install each pivot bolt separately.
- Rotate both legs through their full travel to confirm clearance.
- Return legs to the correct position for mating the platform to the cabinet.

### Scene 11 — Install cabinet pivot brackets (2:16–2:29)

- Close-up on the left bracket: bracket, screws, washer, bolt, and locknut.
- Repeat on the right using a mirrored camera angle.
- Use partial transparency to show the pivot axis through both cabinet sides.

### Scene 12 — Mate bed platform to cabinet (2:29–2:41)

- Raise and align the moving platform between the cabinet uprights.
- Slide left and right pivot bolts along their true axes.
- Tighten locknuts without hiding the connection.
- Perform a small 10–15 degree pivot test.

### Scene 13 — Install dual gas pistons (2:41–2:52)

- Attach each piston to its cabinet-side mount.
- Attach each rod end to the moving bed frame.
- Show left and right sides separately.
- Avoid depicting piston compression values or preload unless verified by the manual.

### Scene 14 — Motion and safety validation (2:52–3:03)

- Lower the empty platform slowly.
- Confirm both legs unfold and contact the floor.
- Check that the platform is level and clears the side-storage module.
- Raise it again and confirm that the facade sits flush with the cabinet.

### Scene 15 — Mattress and final demonstration (3:03–3:15)

- Place a Queen mattress no thicker than 8 in.
- Show one complete open cycle and one complete close cycle.
- End with closed, open, and furnished-room hero views.
- English end card: `Reconstructed 3D assembly visualization — consult the official manual before installation.`

## Camera and edit grammar inherited from the reference video

- Wide shot for each major subassembly.
- Top-down view for frame layout and slat placement.
- Tight macro shot for every bracket, hinge, piston, bolt, washer, and nut.
- Parts move one at a time; no complete subassembly should appear without showing its build.
- Hold each completed state for 0.5–1.0 seconds before the next cut.
- Use hard cuts between work areas and smooth camera moves within a single assembly action.
- Use neutral white studio lighting during assembly and a furnished room only for the opening and final demonstration.
- English labels and narration throughout.

## Implementation gate

2. All moving-bed parts share one pivot hierarchy.
3. Both legs use independent hinge pivots.
4. Both piston endpoints remain connected throughout the motion range.
5. The open bed clears the lower crossbar and side storage.
6. Closed depth remains consistent with 15.7 in.
7. Open depth remains consistent with 82 in.
