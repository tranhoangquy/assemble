Welcome to the Three.js assembly guide for the Murphy bed with a folding desk. [[slnc 800]]

Before starting, identify every panel, fastener, hinge, and pivot component. Assemble the product on a level surface, with at least two adults assisting. [[slnc 1000]]

First, build the outer cabinet. Connect the base, vertical side panels, upper rails, and shelving section. Keep the fasteners slightly loose until the cabinet is square, then tighten them evenly. [[slnc 1200]]

Next, assemble the bed frame. Fit the side rails, cross members, support deck, and front panels in the orientation shown. Lift the completed frame into the cabinet and align both pivot points. [[slnc 1000]]

Install the pivot hardware and secure each side. Check that the frame moves smoothly without rubbing the cabinet. [[slnc 900]]

Now attach the folding desk. Fix the desktop to its hinge rail, then install both support legs and their pivot fasteners. Confirm that the desk stays level in the working position. [[slnc 1000]]

Test the mechanism slowly. As the bed opens, the desktop remains horizontal and the support legs fold clear. Close the bed again and verify all clearances. [[slnc 900]]

Finally, anchor the cabinet to structural wall studs or solid masonry using the correct hardware. Never operate an unsecured Murphy bed. Recheck every fastener before use. Assembly is complete.
