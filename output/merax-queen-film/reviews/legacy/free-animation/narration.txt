Before assembly, check every panel, fastener, hinge, safety latch, and gas piston. This heavy wall bed requires at least four adults. Build the cabinet on a protected, level floor. Join the two side columns with the top rail, lower rail, and center supports. Keep every connection slightly loose until the cabinet is square.

Assemble the desk box, then attach it to the cabinet using the long hinge and pivot hardware. Build the foldable desktop and test its travel before tightening the screws.

Next, assemble the bed frame on the floor. Install the cross rails, wooden mattress slats, support legs, and mattress straps. With helpers holding both sides, connect the bed frame to the cabinet. Fit the gas pistons only in the orientation shown in the manual.

Move the unit into its final position. Level the cabinet, locate solid concrete or structural wood studs, and install the correct wall anchors. Never attach this product to hollow drywall alone.

Finally, tighten every fastener, engage the safety latches, and test the desk and bed slowly. Keep hands clear of hinges and do not use the bed until every wall anchor has been inspected.
