# SHORT-FORM ASSEMBLY VIDEO PRODUCTION STANDARD
Version: 1.0
Scope: furniture assembly Shorts in this repository. MUST/REQUIRE are obligations; recommended timings are defaults, not product business rules. A Director override must be explicit and cannot silently falsify validated mechanics.

## Shared authority and presentation
1. Short is a separate presentation/director edit over the validated product and assembly truth. It MUST share product data, AssemblyGraph, geometry/materials, mechanical state and generic engine behavior with Long. Source authority, installation/support/contact/pivot truth, fit, debug cleanliness and approval/provenance requirements from [the shared Long production standard](long-form-assembly-video.md) remain applicable. A Short MUST NOT become an independent product/assembly implementation, globally accelerated Long, or landscape crop.
2. Editorial omission of repeated work MUST preserve true assembly semantics: prerequisites, secured/support states, hardware relationships and moving mechanisms. A representative connection can stand for a declared repetition; cuts MUST NOT hide impossible installation or imply uninstalled structure is secured. Presentation-only preview/movement MUST NOT mutate AssemblyGraph semantics.
3. Each Short/revision MUST have a distinct presentation/video ID, shot plan, portrait camera/caption plan, creative fingerprint and render/checkpoint identity. Compatible shared assets do not imply compatible Long/Short checkpoints.

## Native profile and story
4. The primary production profile is native **1080×1920 at 30fps**. Duration MUST NOT exceed **60s**; approximately **50–58s** is preferred when the story needs it. Production MUST use an authored portrait camera and native output, not landscape rendering followed by cropping. Expected frame count and resulting duration MUST be explicit and consistent with the shared export frame contract.
5. Compression MAY omit repeated connections, show representative connections, use simultaneous/staggered arrivals, shorten holds, editorial cuts, macros and authored source-time windows. Important instructional actions MUST remain readable and mechanically valid. A global playback-speed multiplier MUST NOT be the primary compression method.
6. The opening approximately **2s** MUST convey product value/function immediately. Transformable furniture should show function/transformation where appropriate. A finished-state hook MAY precede chronological assembly, but subsequent assembly MUST restore/evaluate canonical source state deterministically; hook state MUST NOT leak into it.
7. A product-specific story MUST account for functional hook, major-group orientation, compressed major assembly, critical structural connection, important hardware/mechanism, functional proof where relevant, completion, functional payoff and final hero. Responsibilities MAY share shots. WF311613 timestamps MUST NOT become universal rules.
8. Moving furniture SHOULD show useful cause→effect: installed mechanism followed by short valid functional motion. Editorial demonstration MUST evaluate valid source state, retain connected hardware/support and deterministically restore/seek afterward. Final payoff SHOULD show completed usable function; final hero MUST communicate the finished usable product.

## Phone readability
9. Portrait framing MUST keep the active operation understandable on a phone. Context/wide views orient, medium views advance assembly and macros teach critical connections/hardware. Where a macro would confuse location, context→macro→result MUST restore orientation. Peripheral cropping is acceptable only when instructional meaning remains clear.
10. Captions MUST be concise, mobile-readable, evaluated deterministically, and within a declared/verified safe area for the chosen profile. They MUST describe the current beat; long text MUST NOT compensate for a picture that fails to communicate the operation.

## Determinism and export
11. Same timestamp MUST yield the same canonical source/mechanical state, camera and caption, independent of seek direction/history. Editorial windows, hook reset and functional proof MUST preserve forward/backward seek equivalence and restore canonical state without hidden mutation.
12. Short MUST use the existing shared Generate File/resumable exporter, not a separate exporter. [Shared export reliability](../docs/resumable-export.md) applies at portrait resolution: deterministic continuous frame indices, chunked work, persistent checkpoints, validation, bounded retry, renderer recycling and resume. Final encoding requires all expected valid contiguous native frames; a cancelled/failed run is not a final master.
13. Each Director candidate MUST use an isolated revision workspace; prior evidence MUST NOT be overwritten. Conceptual examples are `short/<candidate>-visual-review-v1/`, `short/<candidate>-music-pass01/` and `short/final-<candidate>-1080p/`, not mandatory product IDs.

## Approval and audio
14. **Gate A — Visual:** the full candidate MUST be silent and have actual visual/technical evidence and `REQUIRES DIRECTOR REVIEW` status. Audio work requires explicit visual approval. Approved picture hash, pixels and timing become immutable through audio work.
15. **Gate B — Music:** a Short-specific arrangement MAY reuse approved channel/product music with inherited verified source identity, rights/provenance and credits. Music MUST fit frozen picture; picture MUST NOT be retimed for music. A review mux MUST preserve approved visuals. Music direction approval is separate from numeric media QA.
16. Default audio is music-first/music-only. Narration, SFX, ambience, generated replacements or additional instruments require explicit authorization. Source trims, musical cuts/crossfades, conservative documented music-only time adjustment and smooth gains MAY support the Short. No unapproved new song/version or blind looping.
17. **Gate C — Final master:** music direction approval plus authorization for the mastering scope MUST precede final mastering/mux. Mobile consistency SHOULD preserve musical dynamics, useful hero presence and a clean exact endpoint; compression/limiting/gains/fades MUST remain within authorized scope. Unapproved processing after an exact audio lock requires a new authorization/revision. Actual listening and voice checks MUST be distinguished from numeric measurements; unavailable listening is `NOT RUN` / `VOICE_CONTENT_REQUIRES_DIRECTOR_LISTENING`, never a fabricated PASS. Final designation follows the Director's explicit gate and mandatory technical QA, not an inferred approval.
18. Final verification MUST cover profile, FPS, expected frame count/duration, codec/pixel format, full A/V decode, faststart, starts/alignment/drift, audio rate/channels/loudness/true peak/clipping/internal silence, picture identity/equivalence and protected assets. Video stream copy is preferred; ordered payload/timing/codec evidence and decoded equivalence MUST substantiate identity or state a precise exception. AAC priming/padding MUST be distinguished from intended timeline. Only successful applicable checks and authorization allow `FINAL MASTER COMPLETE`.

## Freeze and retention
19. Accepted/frozen final masters MUST remain immutable in place with SHA256, approval, provenance, required credits and reproducibility information preserved. Changes create new versioned artifacts. Publication/upload is a separate authorization.
20. Disposable cleanup MUST follow [production artifact retention](production-artifact-retention.md). Compact Director/QA/freeze history remains; no automated retention timer or blanket removal of previous revisions is implied.
