# Production artifact retention
Version: 1.0
Scope: authorized cleanup after final Long/Short media is frozen. This policy extends shared production isolation; it does not authorize deleting assets by itself.

1. Cleanup MUST inventory candidates and classify each as **KEEP — protected/reproducible/final**, **KEEP — historical evidence**, or **DELETE — disposable**, with reason, count and logical bytes. Unclear dependencies or active resumable work remain KEEP until resolved.
2. Protected assets MUST include application/product/assembly source and tests, rules/skills, frozen final masters and archival audio, approved relevant music sources, provenance/licenses/credits, approvals/freeze hashes, final creative plan, media verification and minimum working reproduction scripts/configuration/inputs. A large file is not disposable merely because it is large or impedes a push.
3. Historical retention MUST explain major Director choices and regression guarantees with reports, source/candidate hashes, manifests and adequate representative visual/audio evidence where required by the applicable standard. Retaining compact evidence is preferred over redundant full sequences; deleting every older revision is forbidden.
4. Raw frames of completed/frozen jobs, obsolete/cancelled smoke caches, redundant diagnostic screenshots/contact sheets, obsolete probes, temporary FFmpeg/decoded files, rejected/unlevelled iterations, redundant previews/clips, regenerateable analysis arrays and empty logs MAY be disposable only after dependency/evidence review. Approval-critical or script-required excerpts remain protected. Renderer caches are not assumed disposable while a job is active or intended for resume.
5. Exact-path deletion manifest and protected-hash baseline MUST exist before deletion. Symlinks, unrelated products and concurrent writers MUST be accounted for; a scope change requires re-audit. Deletion MUST be limited to classified files with a before-delete identity check. Protected hashes MUST match afterward.
6. Cleanup MUST record actual removed files/bytes/directories, before/after output logical size, checks and Git delta. Lightweight regression/type checks MUST be appropriate to unchanged runtime. Destructive Git history operations, `git gc` and LFS-policy changes are outside cleanup scope.
7. Freeze/provenance records and historical SHA manifests MUST NOT be silently rewritten to pretend purged media still exists. A retention/deletion ledger MUST identify intentional removals, original hash/path, retained replacement/evidence and effect on resume/reproduction. Historical manifests describe their original snapshots; current retained verification is separate. Any required current master bundle MUST stay verifiable.
8. Manual cache purging MUST leave an honest lifecycle: purged raw frames cannot satisfy a future resume without fresh validation/re-render. Completed-master evidence remains valid, but purged cache does not establish fresh frame availability. Do not change exporter behavior, architecture or automatic deletion policy as a side effect of cleanup.
