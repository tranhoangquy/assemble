# Canonical connection recipes

Read for connections actually present in the source. These are teaching procedures, not manufacturer torque, substrate or fastener specifications. Omit inapplicable stages with rationale; retain support during every operation.

## Dowel
- **Staging:** Stage dowel and receiving members separately; show receiving bores.
- **Alignment:** Match axes/depths and correct receiving faces.
- **Insertion:** Insert along bore axis, then place mating member along permitted path.
- **Close-up:** Bore mouth and seating contact.
- **Securing:** Use specified seated dowel/mating connection; no invented tightening.
- **Verification:** Seat/depth, mating fit and quantity.
- **Camera needs:** Axial detail plus mating-face view.
- **Common failure modes:** Wrong bore face, exposed accidental through-mouth, doubled dowel, penetration.

## Cam
- **Staging:** Stage cam, specified mating stud and members in source order.
- **Alignment:** Show cam orientation and stud capture axis.
- **Insertion:** Seat cam/stud and join members through accessible paths.
- **Close-up:** Cam access and capture interface.
- **Securing:** Turn cam using accessible driver as specified; do not rotate panel to fake locking.
- **Verification:** Capture, panel contact, cam quantity and retained access.
- **Camera needs:** Access-face close-up plus stud axis.
- **Common failure modes:** Wrong cam orientation, closed driver access, invented bolt/cam order.

## Screw
- **Staging:** Stage screw/tool and show intended pilot/receiving face.
- **Alignment:** Align screw/driver with source axis.
- **Insertion:** Translate into pilot while rotating when appropriate.
- **Close-up:** Head, pilot and receiving stack.
- **Securing:** Seat head per source; no invented torque.
- **Verification:** No excess penetration, correct depth/count and tool clearance.
- **Camera needs:** Axis detail and head seating.
- **Common failure modes:** Floating screw, wrong length, blocked tool, far-face breach.

## Bolt
- **Staging:** Stage bolt and specified threaded receiver.
- **Alignment:** Align hole stack/receiver and tool axis.
- **Insertion:** Pass bolt through specified stack without clipping.
- **Close-up:** Receiver and head contact.
- **Securing:** Tighten into source-defined receiver; show compatible restraint if needed.
- **Verification:** Engagement, seating/count and retained mechanism freedom.
- **Camera needs:** Stack/axis detail.
- **Common failure modes:** Wrong receiver, missing access, seized moving joint, misaligned stack.

## Bolt + washer + nut
- **Staging:** Stage specified components; derive stack and order from source.
- **Alignment:** Align stack and receiving axes.
- **Insertion:** Insert bolt and place washers/nut on correct faces in approved sequence.
- **Close-up:** Both faces and stack.
- **Securing:** Show plausible tool/counterhold access; tighten as specified.
- **Verification:** Washer/nut counts, engagement, seating and moving clearance.
- **Camera needs:** Opposite-face views and tool access.
- **Common failure modes:** Wrong washer face/order, doubled stack, nut before accessible insertion.

## Bracket
- **Staging:** Stage bracket against required mating faces and fasteners.
- **Alignment:** Align bracket orientation, faces and holes.
- **Insertion:** Seat bracket without hiding intersections; insert specified hardware.
- **Close-up:** Bracket faces and fastener axes.
- **Securing:** Secure per source while maintaining support/access.
- **Verification:** Face contact, correct holes/count and structural support.
- **Camera needs:** Corner/inside detail plus pullback.
- **Common failure modes:** Floating bracket, wrong handedness, fastener path blocked.

## Pivot / bearing
- **Staging:** Stage bearing/shaft and source-defined support.
- **Alignment:** Align true pivot axis with hierarchy and receiver.
- **Insertion:** Seat/capture shaft or bearing along accessible path.
- **Close-up:** Bearing capture and axis.
- **Securing:** Apply specified retention without locking required freedom.
- **Verification:** Rotate through intended range; inspect support/collision and clearances.
- **Camera needs:** Axis detail plus functional medium-wide.
- **Common failure modes:** Orbit around wrong point, unsupported pivot, invalid axis, missing retention.

## Piston / gas strut
- **Staging:** Stage linkage ends while mechanism is supported.
- **Alignment:** Align mount axes and source-defined end orientation.
- **Insertion:** Attach accessible ends in source order.
- **Close-up:** End capture, mount and telescoping body.
- **Securing:** Secure retention per source; preserve articulating ends.
- **Verification:** Stroke/range, clearance, support and endpoint engagement.
- **Camera needs:** Mount close-ups and mechanism sweep.
- **Common failure modes:** Wrong mounting side, fixed-length fake linkage, overstroke, unsafe unsupported pose.

## Leg / support
- **Staging:** Stage support with host assembly supported.
- **Alignment:** Align feet, joints and source-defined pivot/fixed axes.
- **Insertion:** Seat or attach via approved route and fasteners.
- **Close-up:** Joint, foot contact and retainer.
- **Securing:** Secure without preventing intended fold/motion.
- **Verification:** Ground contact, load support, motion and count.
- **Camera needs:** Underside joint then full grounded view.
- **Common failure modes:** Floating foot, implausible support, wrong fold axis, premature load transfer.

## Wall anchor
- **Staging:** Stage source-specified bracket/anchor with explicit installation surface assumptions.
- **Alignment:** Align structural fixing positions and access.
- **Insertion:** Place bracket/anchor per source; do not invent substrate-specific details.
- **Close-up:** Wall interface and tool paths.
- **Securing:** Show approved fastening; document unknown substrate/anchor decisions.
- **Verification:** Wall contact, required engagement/count and supported product.
- **Camera needs:** Wall-side detail and grounded full view.
- **Common failure modes:** Anchor floating, wrong surface, invented substrate suitability, inaccessible driver.
